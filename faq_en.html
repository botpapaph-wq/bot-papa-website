Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is BOT PAPA for?

BOT PAPA is designed for trades and self‑employed service providers in the DACH region who spend most of their day on job sites, with customers or in the studio – and still need to stay reachable.

Typical examples: painters, roofers, plumbers, metalworkers, smaller service providers, coaches and personal trainers. Law firms and medical practices are deliberately not part of my focus.

What does it cost to get started?

The first step is a compact starter guide as a PDF. It costs 2.99 USD – not as a “product price” but as a handling and processing fee for the transaction.

The basic setup of an individual AI phone assistant (3 hours) is a flat 299 EUR. Every additional hour that becomes necessary due to later requests or extra scope is billed at 49 EUR.

For ongoing maintenance and small changes there is a maintenance plan: 49 EUR per month, regardless of whether changes are needed in a given month – cancellable on a monthly basis.

What is included in the 3‑hour setup?

The basic setup usually includes:

  • a short needs assessment,
  • prompting and building a custom behavior prompt for your voice assistant,
  • entering your key knowledge (services, processes, location, opening hours),
  • backend setup such as SMS and/or email notifications, routing and basic logic.

The goal is an assistant that greets in your tone of voice, handles calls in a structured way and produces callback‑ready notes – no hype, but practical.

Which phone systems are supported?

For the DACH region I primarily work with Placetel. If you are not using Placetel yet, the first step is often switching to that provider – I support you with this.

Alternatively, I can also work with setups based on Telnyx. I do not sell my own phone systems – I design and maintain tailored AI voice assistants on top of existing systems.

Do you sell your “own” AI like Placetel AI?

No. Placetel AI is a standalone product from Placetel, directly integrated into the Placetel platform as an AI phone assistant. It can book appointments, qualify inquiries and pass cases to connected systems.[web:37][web:40]

BOT PAPA builds on such platforms, translates your business into clear conversation logic and takes care of custom setup, adjustments and ongoing maintenance – especially for smaller businesses that cannot handle all this “on the side”.

Doesn’t this feel impersonal?

An AI assistant does not replace an owner or master craftsperson on the phone. But it does make sure that prospects get a response at all, instead of ending up on voicemail forever or never getting through.

The language is deliberately natural, without marketing buzzwords. The assistant clearly introduces itself as an AI receptionist and does not try to “pretend” to be a human.

Will the assistant really understand my customers?

The assistant uses modern language models and is designed to understand everyday language – including job‑site slang and very direct wording.

I work with real conversation examples and adjust the prompt so that typical requests in your trade are captured and structured cleanly. If something is not understood, the assistant asks openly – instead of guessing.

What happens to calls and data?

Technically, calls can be recorded and transcripts can be created – whether this is used depends on your preferences and the legal framework in your country.

As a rule: you, as the operator, are responsible for legally compliant information to callers, consents and notices. I support you with practical wording, but I do not replace legal advice.

Is this even legal?

Using an AI phone assistant is generally permitted if you are transparent, have an appropriate legal basis for processing data and work properly with your providers (for example data processing agreements, GDPR compliance).

Important topics include informing callers, handling recordings and the choice of services used. For concrete legal questions you should always involve your tax or legal advisor.

What can the assistant explicitly not do?

The assistant does not provide legal or tax advice and does not give binding medical statements. These topics must always be handled by qualified people.

Emergencies, medical issues, legal questions or escalated complaints should either not be handled by the AI at all or be handed over quickly to a human – I take this into account when designing the conversation logic.

When is BOT PAPA rather not worth it?

If you only receive a handful of calls per week that you can easily handle yourself or with a classic voicemail, an AI receptionist usually does not pay off.

It becomes interesting where calls come in regularly, but between scaffolding, job sites and customer appointments you simply cannot answer every call personally – and still do not want to miss out on any opportunities.

What’s the best way to get started?

The simplest entry is a short, focused initial call. We look at your current phone setup, typical call situations and reasonable tasks for the assistant – without sales pressure.

If it makes sense for both sides, the next step is the starter guide and then the 3‑hour setup for your first production bot.
